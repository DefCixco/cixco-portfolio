Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build from scratch or use templates?

I build custom systems tailored to your specific logic. While I have a library of proven components (to save time), the architecture is always unique to your business needs.

What is your typical turnaround time?

For standard automation workflows, 3-5 days. For full GoHighLevel ecosystem setups, typically 10-14 days. I prioritize quality over rushing.

Do you offer support after the project is done?

Yes. Every project includes a 14-day "Hyper-Care" period where I monitor the system and fix any bugs for free. Ongoing maintenance packages are also available.

Who do you typically work with?

I mostly work with coaches, consultants, founders, agencies, and e-commerce teams who need their operations to run more smoothly. If your business relies on tools like CRMs, automation, or multiple systems talking to each other, I’m likely a good fit.

Do you only work with specific tools?

I primarily work with Make, GoHighLevel, n8n, Zapier, and Airtable. That said, my focus is on system design and logic — not just tools. If a project requires learning or integrating something new, I’m comfortable adapting where it makes sense.

Can you work with existing systems or messy setups?

Yes. A lot of my work involves reviewing and improving existing workflows that have become difficult to manage. I take time to understand what’s already in place before making changes or rebuilding where necessary.

Do you offer documentation or handover support?

Yes. I document workflows and explain how things work so you’re not left guessing after delivery. The goal is for you or your team to feel confident managing the system going forward.

What if I’m not sure what I need yet?

That’s completely fine. Many clients come in with a problem, not a solution. Part of my role is helping you clarify what makes sense before anything is built.
